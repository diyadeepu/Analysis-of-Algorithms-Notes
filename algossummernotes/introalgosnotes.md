# Introduction to Algorithms Notes
##### * Content from Abdul Bari's Youtube Series on Algorithms *

### What is an algorithm?
A step by step procedure for solving a computational problem.

In the phases of developing a software project, there are two important phases: **_design_** and **_implementation_**.
* Whatever you're trying to manufacture / construct by engineering procedures, make your design perfect and thorough to get a clear picture of what you want to build or develop.
* You can't build anything simply from trial and error. First **_design_**, then write the **_program_**.

### What's the difference between an algorithm and a program?
While both are step by step procedures to solve a computational problem, algorithms are written at the **_design phase_**, while programs are written at the **_implementation phase_**.
